About Us

Made With Love in Pasir Gudang

Ben's Fry is a neighbourhood fried chicken spot in Taman Kota Masai, serving crispy, juicy chicken, burgers and wraps at prices the whole family can enjoy.

Every order is fried fresh, packed with flavour and served with a smile. Whether it's a quick lunch, a family dinner or a late-night craving, we've got you covered.

  • Fried Fresh

    Cooked to order so every bite stays hot and crunchy.

  • Crispy & Juicy

    Golden on the outside, tender on the inside.

  • Family-Friendly Prices

    Sets from RM 8.90 and family combos for 4–8 people.

  • Made With Love
